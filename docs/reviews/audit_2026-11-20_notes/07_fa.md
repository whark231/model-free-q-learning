# 07 Function approximation (working notes)

**TL;DR / In plain terms:** the FA extension replaces the table with a linear model of Q in features of the weights (quadratic, or splines), trained with a constant step tuned on separate seeds. On the 20% grid it roughly matches the table at γ = 5 and misses at γ = 8. On the 10% grid it is far worse than the table. All of it is post hoc and labelled so.

Sources: `docs/methods/FA_INTEGRATION_NOTES.md`, `EXPERIMENTS.md` §8, `src/fa_agent.py`, `src/features.py`, `src/tune_fa.py`, `results/tuning/`.

## Design
- Q̂(a) = θ·φ(w_a). Quadratic features: 45 columns. Spline features: 909 columns, rank 369 on the 20% grid.
- Constant α. Tuned on master seed 20261103 at γ = 5 only: tabular-const 3.2e-4, quadratic 3.2e-4, spline 7.11e-6 (each 1.6e-4 of the bound 2/max‖φ‖²). 10% grid spline: 2.778e-5, noise-matched and not re-tuned.
- Final policy: lowest-index argmax of the final float64 Q values (Albert's fix, 2026-11-04).

## Caveats the report must carry
- Post hoc relative to `baseline-v1`; α tuned at γ = 5 only, on separate seeds.
- The fair comparison for FA is `tabular-const` (same constant-α rule), not the 1/N baseline.
- The ΔQ rule is uninformative under a constant α.
- γ = 8 miss: suspected cause is directions that have not finished learning (uniform exploration lasts about 1.41M episodes; 36 of 45 quadratic and 351 of 369 spline directions unfinished). The supporting calculations (surrogate study, mean-field calculation, shrinkage slopes from `run_000.npz`) have no committed script. Report as a hypothesis.
- Median Spearman of Q fit is high for FA on the 10% grid (0.976) while the chosen portfolio is poor: good ranking overall, wrong at the top (top-10 recall 0.00).
