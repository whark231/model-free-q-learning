## TL;DR / In plain terms

The core project (data, simulated market, benchmarks, action grid, reward, tabular agent, evaluation, reports) is correct: four independent reviewers re-derived its numbers by different methods and found no correctness bug. The function-approximation (FA) code is also correct, but the predictions registered for the three constant-learning-rate runs mostly failed (four of five checks, in whole or in part), and the report has to say so. The transaction-cost (TC) extension has no code bug but cannot learn as designed: its trained policy scores the same as picking a random grid portfolio, so it needs a redesign before any more TC runs.

**Key concepts to understand**

- **A bug versus a design flaw.** A bug means the code does something other than what it says. A design flaw means the code does what it says, but what it says cannot work. The TC extension is the second kind: the reviewer ran the unmodified training loop on a small grid with exact rewards and it matched the oracle within 0.3 bps/yr, so the loop is right; with the real problem's size and noise it learns nothing.
- **Shrinkage under a constant learning rate.** With α constant and Q starting at 0, a portfolio updated N times holds about (1 − (1 − α)^N) of its true value. Portfolios tried only during exploration (about 1,100 times each) end at about 0.3 of their value. This shrinkage is measured; that it is why `tabular-const` met the convergence rule while picking the wrong portfolio is an inference, not tested by a counterfactual run.
- **False "changed" flags.** Files hashed on Windows have CRLF line endings and on the Mac LF, so the same file gets two different hashes. Every "calibration changed" flag in the reports is this artifact.

**Key takeaways**

- Nothing found invalidates `baseline-v1` or the tabular post-hoc runs. Parts I to VI of the required work stand.
- The FA extension has results worth reporting, as long as the report states which registered predictions failed (`EXPERIMENTS.md` §8.7).
- The TC extension, as run, would lose points under "correctly designed, benchmarked, and interpreted". Do not run the two registered TC comparisons as designed. §6 lists the options.
- Most open items are small code fixes (Windows crash, line-ending hashing, report fields) that change no number.

### 2.3 H4 closed (William, 2026-11-10): the constant-α experiment and its failed expectations

**TL;DR / In plain terms:** H4 asked for the failed predictions of the three constant-learning-rate runs to be written down plainly, next to the experiment they belong to. This section is that write-up, in a form the FA section of the report can use directly. Every number below was recomputed on 2026-11-10 from the committed `summary.json` and `per_seed.csv` files, or from the training logs where stated, and matches `EXPERIMENTS.md` §8.7.

**Key concepts to understand**

- **Shrinkage.** With a constant step size α and a value starting at 0, a portfolio updated N times holds about 1 − (1 − α)^N of its true value. A portfolio that is never chosen after exploration ends shrunk about 70% toward 0: below its true value when that value is positive, above it when negative (393, 79 and 818 of the 1,287 portfolios at γ = 5, 3 and 8).
- **Directions.** A linear model θ·φ learns along the eigenvectors of the feature matrix E[φφᵀ]. A direction with eigenvalue λ closes its gap from θ = 0 at about α·λ per uniformly explored episode, so after N such episodes it has closed about 1 − e^(−α·λ·N) of its gap. A direction with α·λ·N < 1 has closed less than 63% and has not finished learning. The 36 slow quadratic directions have α·λ·N between 0.26 and 0.31 (23 to 26% closed); 307 of the 369 spline directions are below 0.1.

**The experiment.**

- **Question.** With the same constant learning rate, does a linear Q-function learn the reward landscape better than a table?
- **Runs.** `tabular-const`, `fa-quadratic` and `fa-spline`, post hoc. Registered with expected directions on 2026-11-05 before training (`src/config.py`, `EXPERIMENTS.md` §8.3 to §8.5) and run on 2026-11-06. Each run trains 10 seeds at each of γ = 5, 3 and 8.
- **Settings shared with `baseline-v1`.**
  - The 20% grid (1,287 portfolios) and 13M episodes of B = 20 days.
  - ε starts at 1 and decays exponentially to 1% at the halfway point (β = 7.08e-7).
  - Values and θ start at 0, and the discount is 0.
  - Master seed 20261016, so seed i sees the same returns in every run.
- **Learning rates.** Each is 1.6e-4 of its model's stability bound 2 / max‖φ‖²:
  - tabular: 3.2e-4;
  - quadratic: 3.2e-4;
  - spline: 7.11e-6.

  `src/tune_fa.py` chose them on master seed 20261103, at γ = 5 only (M12).
- **Models.**
  - **Quadratic.** The 45 products w_i·w_j, which can represent the mean-variance reward exactly.
  - **Spline.** Additive cubic B-splines per asset plus pairwise tensor splines: 909 generic features, built without the reward's form, that span 369 directions on the 20% grid.

  Both models refresh the greedy portfolio every 100 updates.
- **Comparisons.** Paired by seed against `tabular-const` (same α) and `baseline-v1` (α = 1/N). SEs are of the paired mean over 10 seeds; gaps are in bps/yr against the best grid portfolio.

**Registered expectations against the outcome.** The three registered expectation strings split into five checks. One held in full and four failed in whole or in part:

| Run | Expectation | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| `tabular-const` | Larger mean gap than `baseline-v1` at every γ | Met: paired +25.9 ± 11.5, +76.6 ± 24.6, +30.2 ± 8.0 at γ = 5, 3, 8 |
| `tabular-const` | Fewer seeds meeting the primary rule than `baseline-v1` at every γ | Failed at every γ: 10, 8, 10 against 9, 8, 9 |
| `fa-quadratic` | Smaller mean gap than `tabular-const` at every γ | Met at γ = 5 and 3 (−20.9 ± 9.8, −99.2 ± 23.7); failed at γ = 8 (+15.4 ± 6.5) |
| `fa-quadratic` | Higher median Spearman and top-10 recall than `tabular-const` at every γ | Spearman met at every γ (0.915, 0.854, 0.951 against 0.813, 0.703, 0.917). Recall met at γ = 5 and 3 (55%, 80% against 25%, 15%), tied at γ = 8 (20% each) |
| `fa-spline` | Lower Spearman and top-10 recall than `fa-quadratic` at every γ | Failed: Spearman higher at every γ (0.950, 0.945, 0.957). Recall lower, as predicted, at γ = 5 and 3 (45%, 65%), tied at γ = 8 |

**Two registered reasons were wrong.**

1. **`tabular-const`: "a noise floor keeps the greedy portfolio switching."**
   - What was measured is shrinkage. Most portfolios are tried only during exploration, a median of 1,097 times, and 1 − (1 − 3.2e-4)^1,097 = 0.296.
   - In `run_000`, a through-origin regression of final Q on true value, over portfolios with fewer than 5,000 visits, gives slopes of 0.291, 0.291 and 0.296 at γ = 5, 3 and 8. The greedy portfolio holds 1.08, 1.06 and 1.14 of its true value in `run_000`, and 0.69 to 1.14 across all 30 seeds.
   - Inferred, and not tested by a counterfactual run: rivals shrunk to 30% cannot overtake the greedy portfolio once ε fades, so the agent locks in. 28 of 30 seeds met the primary rule, and 2 ended on the best portfolio.
2. **§8.4: "the slowest quadratic direction learns in about 5.5M of the 13M episodes."**
   - That assumed every episode explores uniformly. Uniform exploration actually totals about 1/β = 1.41M episodes.
   - At that count, 36 of the 45 quadratic directions and 351 of the 369 spline directions have α·λ·1.41M < 1.
   - This is the suspected cause of the γ = 8 miss, where every FA seed holds 20% SPY and the best portfolio holds 40%. `fa-quadratic` picks the same portfolio on 8 of 10 seeds. Not tested, with two caveats from `EXPERIMENTS.md` §8.7:
     - a reviewer's mean-field calculation reproduces the spline's γ = 8 pick, but for the quadratic it is far off, so it explains the quadratic's miss only in part;
     - α was tuned at γ = 5 only, which could also contribute at γ = 8.
   - C2 in §6.3 was meant to test it, but with ε at 3% halfway the quadratic still has 36 of 45 slow directions, so C2 as written is probably too weak.

**What the report can claim.**
- Both FA models beat `tabular-const` at γ = 5 and 3: paired `fa-quadratic` − `tabular-const` −20.9 ± 9.8 and −99.2 ± 23.7, `fa-spline` − `tabular-const` −27.8 ± 11.9 and −61.8 ± 24.2. The γ = 5 differences are about 2 SE with 10 seeds, so call them moderate. Both rank the whole landscape better on Spearman at every γ.
- Neither beats `baseline-v1` reliably, and at γ = 8 both are clearly worse. Paired against `baseline-v1` at γ = 5, 3 and 8: `fa-quadratic` +5.0 ± 5.2, −22.6 ± 12.8, +45.7 ± 5.6; `fa-spline` −1.8 ± 9.5, +14.8 ± 17.3, +41.6 ± 6.0. So no "discovery" claim.
- Under a constant α the primary convergence rule tracks stability and says little about accuracy. The ΔQ rule is met at the first checkpoint by every seed: the largest single update in a window is 1e-6 to 5e-6 in `tabular-const`'s `run_000`, under the 1e-5 tolerance from the start.

**How it was re-checked on 2026-11-10.**
- **The §8.7 table and the six paired differences:** recomputed from each experiment's `summary.json` and `per_seed.csv`, and identical.
- **1/β = 1,411,457:** from the β recorded in the summaries.
- **The 36-of-45 and 351-of-369 counts:** re-derived from the eigenvalues of E[φφᵀ] over the uniform 20% grid, using `src/features.py` and the α values in `src/config.py`. The 909 spline features have rank 369 on the grid.
- **The shrinkage slopes and greedy ratios:** recomputed from `tabular-const`'s `run_000.npz` logs on William's machine.
- None of this used μ or Σ during training. They enter only the evaluation, as before.
- An independent reviewer recomputed every number on 2026-11-10 and found none wrong; its wording corrections (shrinkage sign, "not finished" in place of "barely moved", the quadratic caveat, the γ = 8 losses to `baseline-v1`) are applied above.

**Key takeaways**

- The FA section reports the failures as they are: four of five registered checks failed in whole or part, and two registered reasons were wrong.
- FA ranks the landscape better than a table under the same α, but picks no better portfolio than `baseline-v1`, and worse at γ = 8.
- The unfinished-directions explanation is the leading suspect, untested; the 10% grid run will face it too.

## 4. Verified correct

Each by an independent route, not by re-running the implementation.

- **Data.** Rebuilt from `data/raw/` with separate code: 5,152 excess-return days, 2006-02-07 to 2026-07-31, matching `excess_returns.csv` to 7e-16. The French RF is percent per day, divided by 100. μ and Σ (ddof = 1) match to 1e-16, in `TICKERS` order everywhere. Σ's condition number is 268.1.
- **Benchmarks.** Budget-only optimum via the KKT linear system matches the closed form to 2e-15. Long-only optima by exact enumeration of all 511 supports match SLSQP to 5e-7 at γ = 3, 5 and 8.
- **Grid.** 495, 1,287 and 43,758 portfolios; all weights ≥ 0 and summing to 1 within 2.2e-16; built by combinatorics only, with no benchmark input. The best grid portfolio matches brute force on every grid and γ; discretization gaps 2.47, 5.74 and 6.13 bps/yr on the 20% grid.
- **Reward.** E[reward] = μ'w − (γ/2)w'Σw with ddof = 1, by 20M Monte Carlo episodes (z = −1.4; a ddof = 0 bias would have shown at about 17 SE).
- **FA.** Quadratic features: 45 columns, rank 45, max ‖φ‖² = 1. Spline: 909 columns, rank 369, max ‖φ‖² = 45. Both represent U(w) exactly (residual ≤ 1e-11). The SGD step, l2 term, greedy refresh, tie stream and divergence guard are correct. Final Q = Fθ to 1e-19.
- **Evaluation.** Re-derived for all 60 `tabular-const` and `fa-quadratic` seeds from the training logs: learned action, gap, Pearson, Spearman, top-10 recall, optimism and the through-end flag all match.
- **Runs.** Every `settings.json` and manifest matches its `POST_HOC_RUNS` registration and the tuned values in `results/tuning/fa_tuning.json`; all three constant-α runs trained at `bc311a3`. Seeds are paired across runs (identical return streams and ε logs).
